About Samarkand State Medical University

Founded in 1930, Samarkand State Medical University (SSMU) is one of the leading government medical universities in Uzbekistan. It has a long academic reputation and a strong track record of training doctors, with international recognition and accreditation from authorities including the World Health Organization (WHO), ECFMG, the National Medical Commission (NMC) of India, UNESCO and FAIMER.

SSMU offers popular MBBS programs for Indian students, including MBBS (General Medicine) and BDS (Dentistry). The program is designed for a total duration of 6 years, which includes one year of compulsory internship as part of the curriculum.

Tuition fees for the MBBS course are approximately confirmed each intake with hostel accommodation and administrative costs shared upfront — our counsellors will share the current, verified figures for you.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Completed 10+2 (senior secondary education) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology, with at least 50% aggregate (45% for reserved categories) in PCB from a recognized board.
  • Qualified NEET-UG. Indicative NEET cut-off: 147 marks for general category, 113 marks for reserved category.
  • The applicant must be at least 17 years old by 31st December of the admission year.
  • Admission is granted on the basis of scores achieved in the entrance examination (NEET).

NEET cutoff marks and other eligibility criteria are set by the NMC and may change each year — our counsellors will confirm the current requirements for you.

Course Duration & Details

  • Total duration: 6 years (5 years academics + 1 year compulsory internship).
  • Official language medium: English.
  • Degree awarded: MD (equivalent to MBBS).
  • Recognition: WHO, ECFMG, FAIMER & NMC (India) recognized.
  • Clinical exposure: hospital-based clinical training in later years and during the internship.
