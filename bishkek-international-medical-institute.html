About Bishkek International Medical Institute

Bishkek International Medical Institute (BIMI) is a medical institute located in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan, offering an MBBS program taught in English alongside Kyrgyz and Russian. It is one of the destinations Indian students consider for affordable medical education abroad, combining theoretical coursework with clinical training.

Why Choose BIMI?

  • Structured Medical CurriculumA curriculum that blends classroom learning with practical, hands-on clinical training.
  • Experienced FacultyQualified teaching staff supported by labs and infrastructure for medical education.
  • Relatively AffordableGenerally lower tuition and living costs compared to many other study-abroad destinations — ask us for current figures.
  • Recognition StatusOur counsellors verify current NMC/WHO recognition status as part of your admission guidance.
  • Hands-On Clinical ExposurePractical training is built into the medical education structure.
  • International Student CommunityA mix of local and international students studying medicine together.

Location

Bishkek International Medical Institute is located in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Contact our team for the current campus address and directions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, with at least 50% aggregate (general category) or 40% (reserved category).
  • Qualified NEET-UG.
  • Minimum age of 17 years by 31st December of the admission year.

NEET cutoff marks and other eligibility criteria are set by the NMC and may change each year — our counsellors will confirm the current requirements for you.

Course Duration & Details

  • MBBS abroad programs typically run for 6 years, including a compulsory internship.
  • Medium of instruction: English (with additional language support in some cases).
  • Curriculum generally follows an internationally aligned medical education structure.

Exact program structure, credits and duration should be confirmed with our counsellors before you apply.
